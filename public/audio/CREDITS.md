# Original soundtrack credits

Original compositions, motifs, arrangements and rendering script: R太郎と伝説の海鮮丼 project. No existing game melodies, MIDI files or recordings were used.

Rendered with FluidSynth and TimGM6mb SoundFont (2004 Tim Brechbill, contributions 2010 David Bolton). The SoundFont is GPL-2.0; the rendered soundtrack is distributed under GPL-2.0 with LICENSE.txt. The SoundFont is not bundled. Reproduction sources: scripts/render-soundtrack.py and compositions.json. TimGM6mb is available from Debian package timgm6mb-soundfont; upstream https://github.com/musescore/musescore-old/commit/90c33ef9d87b3f5ff92efd3b07d89eb455fb1fef .

15 stereo Ogg Vorbis files, 22050 Hz, source amplitude quantized to 12 bits. Lead, countermelody, string chords, bass, harp and sampled drums. Loop assets render two passes and extract the second pass so the reverb tail wraps. Victory and defeat are non-looping.
